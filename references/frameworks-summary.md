# Frameworks Summary

## Thinking Traps

- **Object thinking -> agency thinking**
  - Stop starting from what others should do
  - Start from what you can choose, influence, or learn
- **Fixed mindset -> growth mindset**
  - Replace “I’m not this kind of person” with “how could I become capable of this”
- **Scoring mindset -> multidimensional thinking**
  - Do not compress complex decisions into one number like pay, title, or prestige

## Craft Mindset

- Learn through practice, feedback, and iteration
- Keep action, reflection, and study in balance
- Learn from people with real results, not only elegant theories

## Financial Freedom Logic

- Wealth comes from the gap between income and spending, compounded over time
- The controllable levers are time, spending, income growth, and return quality
- Avoid FOMO, emotion-driven investing, and areas you do not understand

## Personal Value Formula

- Personal value = understand the market × build the product × use leverage
- Move from effort to output to outcome
- Protect core abilities that compound rather than betting everything on fragile tools

## Dao-Tian-Di-Jiang-Fa Career Choice Lens

- `Dao`: how you think
- `Tian`: trend and timing
- `Di`: platform and business unit
- `Jiang`: manager and allies
- `Fa`: methods and execution

Do not overfocus on `Fa` while the upper layers are wrong.

## Find the 10% That Matters

- Work expands to fill available time
- Low-value tasks flow toward people who tolerate them
- High-value work can be orders of magnitude more important than busywork

## Jungle Capability

- Keep validating your value in the real market
- Do not confuse platform halo with your own portable strength
- Long-term success comes from craft plus sustained compounding
